CFD Fundamentals

Modern CFD codes use the finite volume method to calculate the velocity and pressure of moving fluids. The CFD Fundamentals courses use simple examples to show you the finite volume method in action. You will see how differential equations are transformed into matrices. You will then assemble and solve the matrices yourself, using either Excel, MATLAB or python.

Fundamentals 1 starts by solving the convection (advection) and diffusion of heat in a 1D channel. This example is extended in Fundamentals 2 and 3 to look at 2D solutions, unstructured meshes and wall functions.

CFD Fundamentals is an established series of courses, with over 5000 students enrolling since 2020. They are an essential foundation for further study of advanced topics in CFD.

CFD for Professionals

When engineers are taught CFD, they are often taught how to use a particular CFD code and run a particular simulation. However, they are rarely taught how to get good results and how to present them in a professional manner.

CFD for Professionals teaches verification, validation and presentation, using techniques gathered from over 10 years of CFD experience. These techniques can be applied to any CFD code (ANSYS Fluent, OpenFOAM, Star CCM, etc.) and are used routinely by professional engineers.

CFD for Professionals is divided into two chapters. Chapter 1 shows you how to quantify accuracy using mesh refinement and Richardson Extrapolation - a technique recommended by the Journal of Fluids Engineering. Chapter 1 also covers sources of experimental data, and how to compare turbulence models. Chapter 2 shows you how to present CFD data correctly, using contour plots, line plots and tables.

Worked examples are provided throughout the course in Excel, MATLAB and python, which you can take away and apply to your own simulations!
