CFD Fundamentals

Modern CFD codes use the finite volume method to calculate the velocity and pressure of moving fluids. The CFD Fundamentals courses use simple examples to show you the finite volume method in action. You will see how differential equations are transformed into matrices. You will then assemble and solve the matrices yourself, using either Excel, MATLAB or python.

Fundamentals 1 starts by solving the convection (advection) and diffusion of heat in a 1D channel. This example is extended in Fundamentals 2 and 3 to look at 2D solutions, unstructured meshes and wall functions.

CFD Fundamentals is an established series of courses, with over 5000 students enrolling since 2020. They are an essential foundation for further study of advanced topics in CFD.

CFD for Professionals

Make your work stand out from other CFD engineers. Learn techniques that can signifcicantly improve the quality of your thesis, academic papers and technical reports. This course does not teach you how to carry out CFD analyses, but how to master your results and excel at presenting them.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

CFD for Professionals is delivered using a comprehensive PDF textbook and video lectures. The textbook is divided into 2 Chapters:

  1. Verification and Validation
  2. Presenting Data

Chapter 1 shows you how to demontrate the accuracy of your calculations. You will learn how to carry out a mesh refinement study correctly using Richardson Extrapolation, which is a technique recommended by the Journal of Fluids Engineering. Chapter 1 also shows you how to choose the right RANS turbulence model and find good sources of experimental data for validation.

Chapter 2 covers the most popular methods of presenting data: contour plots, line plots and tables. You will learn simple techniques that can significantly improve the clarity of your data including: how to choose the right colour map on your contour plots and how to use zoom boxes for increased resolution.

Worked examples are provided throughout the course, so that you can learn the techniques as soon as they are presented. Microsoft Excel spreadsheets and Python source code are also provided for Richardson Extrapolation, which you can take away and apply to your own studies!

Professionals shows you how to use modern CFD codes to improve the quality of your simulations. Fundamentals shows you how CFD codes work using the finite volume method.

You can use any CFD code to complete the course, as long as you can run simulations and extract results from it!
